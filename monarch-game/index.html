A Monarch's Beginning

An interactive pixel-art story of a monarch butterfly's first days — egg to caterpillar, told the way it actually happens on a milkweed leaf.

What this is

A short, browser-based comic-and-game hybrid built for this garden project. It follows a single monarch egg from the moment its mother lays it, through hatching, and into a caterpillar's first hungry, dangerous days on the milkweed — instinct, shelter, and the real biology of what "instar 1" actually looks like.
